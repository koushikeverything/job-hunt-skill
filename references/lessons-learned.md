# Lessons learned — method first, then per-archetype appendices

Append new observations under the relevant heading each run. The top half is profession-agnostic METHOD; the appendices hold what specific runs proved.

## The camouflage method — six questions to run ANY profile through (Step 2)

The best find of most runs comes from adjacency, not the direct title list. For each experience block ask:
1. **Which other department owns this work at a bigger company?** (product strategy → founder's office/strategy; L&D → HR ops; casework → trust & safety)
2. **What is this called at a company 10× smaller?** (Head of Product → Founding PM; Head of L&D → "trainer"; CFO → "finance lead")
3. **What is it called at a company 10× bigger?** (accountant → record-to-report analyst; PM → program-family GPM)
4. **Which adjacent function shares ~70% of the skills?** (UX lead → design leadership; nurse → case management/clinical documentation; teacher → edtech content, corporate L&D; visa officer → KYB/BGV/due-diligence)
5. **What do staffing firms, fractional marketplaces, and expert networks call it?** (interim, fractional, consultant — a deliberate labeled slice when intake allows, never mixed silently into employee roles)
6. **What is the government/regulated/institutional variant called?** (PSU, civil-service, hospital-board, school-chain titles)

## Skills-inversion (the second expansion axis)

Search postings by the CV's named artefacts and metrics, not just titles: "design system" + "activation", "triage protocol", "eval harness", "Section 135". This catches roles the market has mislabeled — no title list can.

## Universal verification traps (every archetype, production-proven)

- **Aggregators keep closed roles up for weeks and INVENT eligibility tags.** A role is not verified until an employer-owned surface confirms it. (63% of mirror-sourced "verified" rows needed correction in one audited run.)
- **Remote is lied about three ways:** country-locked postings labeled "remote"; state-enumerated remote that excludes the candidate's state (Twilio/Gujarat); "Remote" tags sitting next to a specific city with no clarifying sentence (ambiguous — ask the recruiter, flag it).
- **Recruiters post as employers.** Search every posting for "recruitment partner", "our client", "on behalf of". The true employer changes the row entirely (ClanX → Savant Labs, + an undisclosed 2:30 AM IST shift in the fine print).
- **Phantom roles exist on aggregators** — titles that collide with another company's real posting (Dentira/Uber). Existence must be confirmed on an employer surface or the row goes to Leads as rung X.
- **Titles inflate and deflate:** "Director" as an internal grade label (Paytm, 3–6 yr band); "Principal" listings with associate-level requirements (Kantata). Verify scope via the requirements line, not the title.
- **Deadlines get invented or go stale.** Only report a deadline you saw on the employer surface. Stale employer-page deadlines with active hiring elsewhere → "rolling — ignore stale deadline".
- Zoho/Workday/Darwinbox/greythr/Keka portals are JS-gated: server-rendered archives on the org's own site, Google-indexed slugs, or mirrors — then verify.
- Email-only applications from small orgs are common and legitimate in some markets; flag `email_only_apply`, advise standard diligence.

## Process lessons (production-proven)

- **One pass is never enough**: 24 → 29 → 29 → 21 new roles across four passes on one profile. Loop until dry.
- **Channel partitioning beats family partitioning**: family-sliced agents duplicated ~12% of fetches on shared sources.
- **Company-first ATS polling is the best quality-per-fetch channel**: 4 of a Top 10 from one Ashby sweep; prolific hirers cluster (7 simultaneous eligible reqs at one company).
- **The adversarial audit pays for itself**: 133k tokens caught an invented deadline, a state exclusion, a misattributed employer, and a phantom role in a single run. Track `audit_correction_rate`; it should fall as gating verification takes hold.
- **Pre-flight one call per source**: a geo-blocked connector burned ~12 calls across three agents before anyone noticed (ZipRecruiter is US/CA-only).
- **Ask for logins at intake**: iimjobs/Instahyre/Cutshort walls discovered mid-run = the largest pool of that run missed.
- **Ask every agent for negative results**; the per-source yield log is what makes run N+1 cheaper.
- Users want the consolidated workbook + the link list; the workbook is the state file.

## Appendix — product-tech (Aug 2026, India)
Seniority scissor: Director/VP at scaled cos bar at 10–15+ yrs; a 6-yr leader's real market is Senior/Lead/GPM/Principal/Founding titles or year-agnostic small companies. Camouflage that worked: Entrepreneur-in-Residence (wants ex-founders!), "Associate Director – Strategy", "Product Manager – Founding Team", Head of Product Operations, design-leadership titles for design-system builders. Salary points: AiPrise SPM ₹40–55L; Vahan Director ₹60–80L + 0.25–1% equity; BMC L/P PM ₹47L mid. CV gaps that recur: AI evaluation vocabulary (eval harness, precision/recall, grounding, guardrails), named AI tooling, SQL, quantified outcomes, explicit years+team-size line.

## Appendix — ngo-development / compliance (Aug 2026, India)
Title camouflage: grants → CSR partnerships/resource mobilization/donor relations; child protection → safeguarding/GBV coordinator/DCPU; visa casework → immigration-consultancy compliance/KYB/BGV/OSINT/trust & safety; KYC → DP & KYC ops/onboarding executive/credentialing QA; training → content & training associate/SME consultant/capacity building. Geography: metro satellites are part of the city market (Ahmedabad ⇒ Gandhinagar/GIFT City; Delhi ⇒ Gurgaon/Noida; Mumbai ⇒ Navi Mumbai/Thane). State language often mandatory in grassroots NGO/govt roles (Gujarati in Gujarat) — near-disqualifying; corporate/BFSI roles in the same city rarely require it. Hard bars: Qualified CS/ICSI, CAMS, clinical licences. Compensation reality: entry compliance/KYC tier-2 ₹20–36k/mo; broking compliance ₹25–65k; CSR ₹40–70k; NGO coordinators ₹25–45k; global-remote L&D evaluators $80–120/hr. Certifications that convert: CAMS/ICA, NISM-VA/SEBI KYC, Articulate 360 sample module, CSR short courses; CS is a multi-year bar — flag, don't recommend.
